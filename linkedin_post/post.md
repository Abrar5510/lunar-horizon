I spent 48 hours walking on Mars. 🚀🔴

This weekend at NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, I built Marswalk solo: a playable helmet-HUD map for an astronaut's first walk on Mount Sharp, in Gale crater.

The ground isn't made up. It's built from NASA's own data:
🛰️ HiRISE 1 m elevation + 25 cm orbital imagery
🌡️ THEMIS thermal inertia
☢️ Curiosity RAD radiation readings
🌬️ Curiosity REMS weather

What it does:
→ Plans a 2 km walking loop that keeps off slopes over 20° and big rocks (29% of the site is no-go)
→ Stops at 4 spots Curiosity actually drilled or photographed, and shows the rover's own photo of each one
→ Tracks oxygen, radiation dose and daylight live in the helmet
→ Throws a solar storm at you, using the real May 2024 event Curiosity measured (dose rate x39), and makes you decide: walk home now, or risk one more core sample?

Built in Unreal Engine 5.8 and streamed live to the browser, so anyone can play it from a link.

My best run: 4 of 4 cores, back 7 minutes before sunset. Score 98/100. 😅

🎮 Play it: https://play.marswalk.earth
